SoundOrbit processes audio in real time so it appears to move around your head as you listen through headphones. It does not modify source files — it only processes sound on its way to your headphones.

Two engines, pick what fits:

* Lightweight (session 0, zero latency, left/right sweep): attaches an effect to the system output mix and modulates left/right gains with an anti-phase triangle wave. No capture, no replay, no echo, no extra buffering. Depth, period, side/center time ratio, and a random period range are adjustable.
* Advanced (capture, pseudo / true surround): captures what other apps are playing, then renders a binaural field with ITD (interaural time difference), ILD (interaural level difference), head-shadow low-pass, and pinna notches. Sources orbit in azimuth, elevation, and distance. Capture and replay introduce some latency; requires a one-time "record screen/audio" permission.

Seven surround modes: pseudo-surround (take left / take right / stereo downmix, each to both ears), and binaural (stereo direct with two independently orbiting sources, take-left source, take-right source, stereo downmix source).

Audio controls (Advanced engine): soft limiter (prevents clipping, on by default), 10-band graphic EQ (ISO octave frequencies, custom presets), and psychoacoustic bass enhancement (off by default). A live visualization switches between a panning view and a top-down head view showing source azimuth, elevation, and motion trail. While surround is on, sound-affecting settings are locked to avoid accidental changes during playback.

Good for: binaural ASMR, adding motion to ordinary music, and streaming audio you cannot download locally.

Privacy: no INTERNET permission, no network calls, no proprietary SDKs. Audio stays on the device — nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent to any external service. Free and open source (GPL-3.0). Requires Android 10+. headphones recommended.
